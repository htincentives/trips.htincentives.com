DAY 1 Basel, Switzerland (Embarkation by 5:00PM): November 29, 2026
  • Stroll through the city center, enjoy the festive illumination and savor Christmas delicacies like waffles, gingerbread & Swiss raclette

Day 2 Breisach, Germany: November 30, 2026
  • Choice of guided tour to explore Freiburg’s medieval city center sheltered by the Black Forest or guided tour of the magical Black Forest to experience local traditions and the vibrant Christmas mood. Afternoon at leisure to explore Breisach’s lovely Christmas market.

Day 3 Strasbourg, France: December 1, 2026
  • Choice of guided City Sightseeing, including the city’s gothic, stained glass cathedral with it’s celebrated ancient murals and astronomical clock, as well as the magical La Petite France District. Your other option is to choose an Optional excursion to a wine country village in Alsace. Afternoon at leisure to stroll through the Christmas markets of Strasbourg.

Day 4 Speyer, Germany 2, 2026
  • Guided tour of Heidelberg’s Castle at Christmastime to see the ruins of the magnificent renaissance treasure towering above the city, and its famed Great Vat-the world’s largest wine barrel. After tour, visit the local German Christmas Markets in Heidelberg.

Day 5 Frankfurt, Germany: December 3, 2026
  • Your cruise ends this morning
